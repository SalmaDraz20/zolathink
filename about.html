AboutZola Think

Learning should prepare young people for real life.

Today, young people have access to more knowledge than ever. But knowing something is not the same as knowing what to do with it.

The world they are growing into is complex, fast-moving, and full of problems that do not come with instructions. That is why we built Zola Think.

THE ZOLA METHOD

We turn learning into real-world experience.

Zola Think puts young people inside realistic Missions. They take on a role, work with a team, make decisions, and create something that has to work in the real world.

The goal is not to teach another tool. It is to help young people know when, why, and how to use what they already know.

BEYOND THE PROJECT

What they build goes beyond the project.

Every Mission is designed to develop skills that matter far beyond the session:

01 / THINK

Critical Thinking

Look closer, question assumptions, and find the signal.

02 / SOLVE

Problem Solving

Turn an unclear challenge into a way forward.

03 / CHOOSE

Decision Making

Make a call when there is no perfect answer.

04 / TOGETHER

Teamwork

Build trust, share the work, and move as one team.

05 / SHARE

Communication

Explain your thinking so other people can use it.

06 / GROW

Confidence

Keep going when the next step is yours to find.

How we see learning.

We do not believe every skill needs to be taught as a separate subject. Mathematics, research, coding, AI, and communication are tools. The real skill is knowing what to do with them.

Where we are going.

We are building a new way for young people to learn: one that feels relevant, challenging, and connected to the world they will enter. Our goal is simple: help young people become confident thinkers who can figure out what to do when there is not a clear answer.

THE STORY BEHIND ZOLA THINK

The story behind Zola Think.

Zola Think started with a simple observation from its founder, Salma Mohamed Draz: young people do not need more information as much as they need more chances to use what they know.

What began as an idea around learning by doing is now being developed into a hands-on learning experience where young people do not just learn something. They have to use it.

Think. Decide. Build. Present. Reflect.
