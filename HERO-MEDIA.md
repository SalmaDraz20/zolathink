# Hero media

The three supplied originals are copied unchanged into `assets/hero-student.png`, `assets/hero-discussion.png`, and `assets/hero-team.png`. The three independent HTML frames in `index.html` use `object-fit: cover` to match the approved collage proportions. Their positions, rotations, borders, layering and responsive scaling live in `hero-media.css`.

Decorative doodles are labeled placeholders. Each has a stable `data-doodle` key: `navy-rays`, `purple-rays`, `small-rays`, `bulb`, `team`, `arrow`, and `bottom-mark`. Replace a placeholder's label with the final asset when supplied, retaining the wrapper class and its geometry. The bulb and team sticker backgrounds are preserved. Text notes remain selectable Arabic HTML. The old `hero-collage.png` is no longer rendered.
