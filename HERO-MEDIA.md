# Hero media

The three supplied originals are copied unchanged into `assets/hero-student.png`, `assets/hero-discussion.png`, and `assets/hero-team.png`. The three independent HTML frames in `index.html` use `object-fit: cover` to match the approved collage proportions. Their positions, rotations, borders, layering and responsive scaling live in `hero-media.css`.

The Hero now uses three lightweight inline SVG doodles: bulb, team, and curved arrow. Their existing wrapper classes preserve percentage positioning, sizes, and rotations. Doodles use thin purple strokes and small yellow accents, without filled backgrounds. The three photos, paper notes, shapes, and collage geometry remain unchanged.
